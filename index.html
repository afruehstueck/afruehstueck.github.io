I am a Research Scientist at Adobe Research London, where I focus on the intersection of computer graphics and generative modeling for image, 3D, and video synthesis.

I earned my PhD in Computer Science from KAUST working with Prof. Peter Wonka.

My work centers on pushing the boundaries of deep neural networks in computer graphics, specifically looking at how we can generate and edit visual content at a large-scale with better quality, finer user control, and seamless, plausible compositing.

Beyond my own research, I am deeply passionate about fostering the next generation of researchers. I am lucky to mentor brilliant students and research interns, and I love staying active in the broader graphics community, e.g. through initiatives like WiGRAPH.

When I’m not deep in code or pixels, I enjoy designing, building, creating, disassembling and assembling things. I love to be outdoors, play music, learn languages and explore the world.
